ATHENA — Goddess of Hope
Soul: 30
Attack: 3
Awaken: 5
________________


Passive — Cosmo of Hope
Whenever one of your Devotees survives an enemy combat, generate 1 Latent Cosmo.
________________


Passive — Athena’s Prayer
Cost: 5 Cosmo
When an Athena Saint you control is destroyed and sent to Tartarus, you may pay 5 Cosmo. If you do, return that Devotee to the battlefield Unprepared with a +1/+1 token on it. You may equip a Cloth to it ignoring equip cost.
________________


Awaken — Seventh Sense (Passive)
All Athena Saints you control get +1/+1.
________________


Awaken Ability — Seventh Sense Protection
Cost: 5 Cosmo
If an Athena Saint you control would be destroyed during combat, you may pay 5 Cosmo. If you do, it survives with 1 Resistance instead.
________________


Ultimate — Athena Exclamation
Cost: 15 Cosmo — Once per game
Destroy all enemy Devotees that are in combat. Your Devotees are not destroyed this way. For each Devotee destroyed this way, its controller loses 1 Soul.
________________


POSEIDON — God of the Seas
Soul: 28
Attack: 3
Awaken: 4
________________


Passive — Frozen Ocean
Cost: 2 Cosmo
When an enemy Devotee declares an attack, you may pay 2 Cosmo. If you do, Sealed that Devotee until the beginning of its controller’s next turn.
________________


Passive — Pressure of the Deep
Whenever you Sealed an enemy Devotee, its controller mills 1 card.
________________


Awaken — Eternal Ice
While Poseidon is Awakened:
Whenever a Sealed enemy Devotee exists, you may pay 2 Cosmo. If you do, choose one:
* Mill 2 cards

* Draw 1 card

________________


Ultimate — Great Flood
Cost: 18 Cosmo — Once per game
Return all Devotees without Cloth to their owners’ hands. Then all players discard their hands and mill 5 cards.
________________
