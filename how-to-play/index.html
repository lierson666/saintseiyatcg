  How to play · Alpha rules

Learn to burn yourCosmo

Saint Seiya TCG is a strategy card game for two players. Each of you commands a God and an army of Devotees: Saints, Marinas, Specters and more. Build your Cosmo, choose your duels, awaken your warriors and bring down the enemy God.

1 · The goal

Defeat the enemy God

Each player controls one God. Gods have Soul instead of HP. When a God's Soul reaches zero, its player loses the match. A player who must draw a card from an empty deck also loses on the spot. If both players lose at the same moment, the match is a draw.

Your Devotees do the fighting. They strike enemy Devotees and the enemy God, protect your own God, and grow stronger with Armor, Awaken and Ultimates.

Every duel matters

Damage stays on your Devotees between rounds. A wounded Saint is still a Saint, until the next blow.

Sacrifice holds the line

Even a mighty warrior can be stopped by Devotees who stand guard and give everything.

Cosmo beats the odds

Cosmo and Armor let weaker warriors overcome stronger foes.

A God is a summit

Bringing down a God is an extraordinary feat. Plan for it.

2 · Read a card

How to read a Devotee

  1. Cost. How much Cosmo you pay to play the card. Devotees and Armor are paid only with Base Cosmo.

  2. Name and subtitle. The character. Effects that name a character only count that exact identity: wearing Pegasus Cloth does not make someone Pegasus Seiya.

  3. Type and subtype. Devotee, Equipment, Technique, Moment or Domain, plus subtypes such as Bronze Saint or Gold Saint.

  4. Abilities and keywords. What the card does. Keywords such as Guardian or Long Range are short rules explained below. When a card's text and a general rule disagree, the card wins.

  5. Power. The damage this Devotee deals in a Strike, both when attacking and when striking back.

  6. HP. How much damage it can hold. When its damage reaches its HP, it is destroyed and goes to the Tartarus.

4 · The five Gods

Choose your divinity

Each God leads a faction with its own style of war. Your God is your life total and your most powerful card. Your Devotees are its only defense.

Soul

Soul is your God's life. At zero, you lose. Gods have no Attack: they never strike and never strike back. Only your board protects your God.

Divine Power

Every God has unique powers printed on its card. Some you activate, some trigger on their own, some are always on. Most can be used once each Round.

Divine Command

Every God also has a Divine Command, a tactical ability for the board. It costs 1 Cosmo; use it in your own round, with an empty stack, once each round. Most Gods have a stronger version while Awakened.

Awaken

A God's Awaken number is a threshold. While its Soul is at or below that number, the God is Awakened and gains extra power. The closer to defeat, the more dangerous a God becomes.

Ultimate

The God's extraordinary move, once per match. Use it in your own round with an empty stack, paying its cost with Base Cosmo. It does not need Awaken: choosing the moment is up to you.

5 · Setting up

Before the first round

Your deck

  • 1 God and 1 Domain, which start in play.
  • A main deck of exactly 50 cards.
  • Up to 3 copies of each card.
  • Cards from your God's faction, plus neutral cards.
  • A Domain from another faction lets you add up to 15 cards of that faction.
  • Not every card is Cosmoble. Keep enough of them, about 40, so you always have Cosmo to grow.

Your Domain

Every Domain has two abilities. The first is the general ability: a small bonus, or an effect worth 1 point at most once each Round. It works with any God. Only one Domain per faction draws a card with it, and always at a price.

The second is marked Resonance with the name of a God. It works only if your God is from the Domain's faction. It is stronger, costs 1 to 3 Cosmo and can be used once each round.

A Domain from another faction

Your Domain matches your God? It works in full. If it belongs to another faction:

  • Its general ability works in full. The Resonance ability is lost.
  • Your deck may hold up to 15 cards of the Domain's faction.

Starting the match

  • Shuffle and draw 7 cards.
  • You may take one free partial mulligan: send back the cards you don't want and draw replacements.
  • A coin toss comes first. Whoever wins chooses to go first or second. Then the players alternate Rounds.
  • The first player doesn't draw in their first round. The other player does.

6 · Cosmo

The energy of the universe within you

Cosmo pays for every card and ability. Cards marked Cosmoble can be placed as a Cosmo Base, once per round. A new Base is ready at once and can pay in the same round. Not every card is Cosmoble: the strongest ones usually aren't, so choosing what to round into Cosmo is part of every round.

Each Base pays 1 Cosmo and stays spent until the start of your next round. Latent Cosmo is a bank of up to 3 that carries over. At the end of your round, every Base you didn't use becomes 1 Latent, so you can answer during your opponent's round.

7 · The round

One player, one Round

The game is played in Rounds. Each Round belongs to one player: the first player takes Round 1, the other takes Round 2, and so on. In your Round you can do as many things as your Cosmo and your Devotees allow.

  1. Start of your round

    Prepare your Devotees, Equipment and Bases. Start-of-round effects happen. Then draw 1 card (the first player skips this in their first round).

  2. Your round

    In any order, and as often as you can pay: place one Cosmo Base, play Devotees and Equipment, play Techniques and Moments, use abilities, and Strike with each Prepared Devotee once.

  3. End Round

    Your unused Bases turn into Latent Cosmo. If you hold more than 7 cards, discard down to 7. End-of-Round effects happen, and effects that last until the end of the Round expire. Then the other player begins a new Round.

Everything can be answered. Each card you play, each ability and each Strike waits on the stack. Your opponent may respond with a Moment, paid with Latent Cosmo. The last thing played resolves first.

8 · Strike

Combat

Declaring a Strike

In your round, each Prepared Devotee can Strike once. Devotees enter play Unprepared, so a new Devotee strikes from your next round on (unless it has Impulse). Striking leaves the attacker Unprepared.

Devotee against Devotee

Both deal damage equal to their Power, at the same time. The defender strikes back even if Unprepared. Even a Devotee destroyed in the clash still deals its damage.

Devotee against God

The Devotee deals its Power to the God's Soul. Gods never strike back: only Devotees can protect a God. An open board leaves your God exposed.

Damage stays

Damage is not healed at the end of the round. A Devotee is destroyed when its damage equals or exceeds its HP, and goes to the Tartarus, where abilities marked Afterlife can still be used.

Every Strike has a price. Defending Devotees always strike back; Gods don't. Before you attack, compare Power and HP on both sides: strike where you survive, finish wounded enemies, use Long Range to hit before the answer, and save your strongest Devotees for the moment that wins the match.

9 · Targets

Who you can hit

Prepared and Unprepared

A Prepared Devotee is ready to Strike. Striking leaves it Unprepared until the start of your next round. Unprepared Devotees still strike back when attacked.

Targets

A Strike can target the enemy God or any enemy Devotee, unless a Guardian is in the way.

Guardian

While your opponent has a Devotee with Guardian in play, Prepared or not, your Strikes must target a Guardian. Nimble ignores Guardians without Nimble. A Nimble Guardian only forces other Nimble Devotees.

Protect your God

Guardians are your wall. Bring them down first, or find a Devotee that can slip past them.

10 · Armor

Cloths, Scales and Surplices

Each faction has its Armor: Athena's Cloth, Poseidon's Scale, Hades's Surplice, Ares's Warplate and Artemis's Moonplate. Each Devotee has one Armor slot; weapons and accessories use their own slots.

Playing Armor

Playing an Armor costs its printed cost, in Base Cosmo only. Its HP adds to the wearer's maximum HP; damage always stays on the Devotee.

Resonance

“Resonance — Pegasus Seiya” links an Armor, weapon or accessory to its character. That character wears it for free when it is played or enters play, and unlocks the abilities marked with Resonance.

Equip

Any Devotee can wear any Armor already in play: pay its printed Equip cost. If the wearer falls, the Armor stays in play, waiting for a new wearer.

Awaken and Ultimate

Awaken belongs to the character, not the Armor. Ultimates are extraordinary abilities with their own conditions, paid only with Base Cosmo.
