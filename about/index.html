A war amongGods

Saint Seiya TCG is a strategic card game. You lead a God and the Devotees who fight in their name. Build up your Cosmo, choose every duel with care, awaken the power of your Saints and bring the enemy God down.

What every match feels like

The game is built around four ideas. If a rule or a card does not serve them, it does not belong.

  • Every duel matters.

    Fights are one against one. Who strikes, who blocks and who falls changes the whole match.

  • Sacrifice can stop giants.

    A legendary Saint is not unbeatable. The right Guardian, at the right time, holds the line.

  • Cosmo beats raw strength.

    Burning your Cosmo and dressing your Devotees in armor lets the underdog win.

  • Defeating a God is legendary.

    Gods hit back. Bringing one down is the hardest thing in the game, and the only way to win.

Five Gods, five ways to fight

Each deck follows one God. Their faction decides how you play: protect, control, endure, attack or hunt.

  • Athena

    The unbreakable will. Saints fight together and grow stronger side by side.

  • Poseidon

    Control and steady pressure, like a rising tide.

  • Hades

    Persistence, sacrifice and the dead who return.

  • Ares

    Aggression, combat and conquest.

  • Artemis

    The hunt: precision and well-chosen eliminations.

A match at a glance

Two players, one God each. The full rules are on How to play.

Your deck

One God, one Domain and a 60-card deck of Devotees, Equipment and Spells.

Cosmo

Play one Base each turn to build the Cosmo you spend on cards and powers.

Rounds

Each Round, both players take a turn. Summon, equip and strike, then end your turn.

Victory

Reduce the enemy God's Soul to zero. Lose your own God, and you lose the match.
