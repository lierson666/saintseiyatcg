Privacy

Your data in the Sanctuary

Saint Seiya TCG is a non-commercial fan project in closed beta. This page explains what we keep, why, and for how long. See also the Terms of use.

What we store

  • Access request: the name and email you send from the home page, and your consent to receive news by email.
  • Account: email, full name, username, country and, if you choose, your phone number.
  • Password: stored only as a one-way hash. We can never read it.
  • Game data: your decks, friends, blocked players, duel challenges and duel results (wins, losses, Gods used).
  • Chat: messages you send to friends. If someone reports you, the recent messages of that conversation are kept with the report for moderation.
  • Security: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and failed sign-in attempts (email and IP address) for 15 minutes to stop password guessing.

Who sees it

Your email, full name and phone are private. Other players see your username, friend code, avatar, online status and your duel record on your profile. The beta team sees account data only to approve access and to handle reports.

Backups

We keep a daily copy of the game database for 14 days to recover from failures. Deleted data disappears from backups within that time.

Your choices

You can edit your profile or delete your account at any time in Profile. Deleting removes your account, sessions, decks, friends, messages and challenges. Your past duels stay in your opponents’ history as “Deleted player”. To stop receiving news, ask the team.

Contact

Questions about your data: write to the Saint Seiya TCG team through the beta channel.
