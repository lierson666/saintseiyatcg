Privacy

Your data in the Sanctuary

Saint Seiya TCG is a fan project in closed beta. This summary explains what we keep and why.

What we store

  • Account: email, full name, username, country and, if you choose, your phone number.
  • Password: stored only as a one-way hash. We can never read it.
  • Game data: your decks and settings.

Who sees it

Your email, full name and phone are private. Other players only see your username, friend code, avatar and online status.

Your choices

You can edit your profile or delete your account at any time in Profile. Deleting removes your account, sessions and decks.

Contact

Questions about your data: write to the Saint Seiya TCG team through the beta channel.
